North America 3D map by Jerry Fisher

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/north-america-3d-map-d55c139774e54037a31048f99b9ce4bb

"At EROS Data Center is a large globe that NASA commissioned to familiarize the astronauts with Earth’s geography. This was in part so the astronauts could figure out where they were while in orbit. I snapped about 59 photos with my LG G4 in manual mode to create this raised relief map. Enjoy!

I didn’t get the best photos in some areas as the globe was rotating. The light was shifting from moment to moment so it made the creation of the model a little tricky."
